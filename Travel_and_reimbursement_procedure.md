# PhD Travel and Reimbursement Procedure

> **Note:** Based on the [June 2026 Mission Regulation](https://www.unitn.it/sites/default/files/2026-06/Regolamento_Missioni_2026.pdf).  
> This checklist sums up the scattered university travel guidelines into a step-by-step guide.

---

## 📋 Phase 1: Before Your Trip

- [ ] **1. Send Email Notification**
  - **To:** `iecs.school@unitn.it`
  - **CC:** Your advisor
  - **Required Details:**
    - Destination (City, Country)
    - Purpose (Conference, summer school, research visit, etc.)
    - Exact start and end dates
    - Funding source / budget code to be used

- [ ] **2. Register Request on Mission Portal**
  - Access the [UniTn Mission Portal](https://www5.unitn.it/Missioni).
  - **Role:** Select the correct one,  **PhD Student**.
  - **Required Attachments:**
    - Event program
    - Official invitation letter / acceptance email

- [ ] **3. Conference / Summer School Fee Payment (via Bank Transfer, called also Wire Transfer)**
  - *Note: Handled via the Eventi Esterni office. Can be initiated simultaneously with Step 2.*
  
  1. **Provide Invoicing Details to Organizers:**
     ```text
     Company: University of Trento
     Address: Via Calepina n. 14
     City/Zip: Trento, 38122
     State/Country: Trentino-Alto Adige, Italy
     VAT Number: IT00340520220
     ```

  2. **Fill out FORM-A:**
     - Download [FORM-A](https://forms.gle/qaJudFN6C6xwTtM69).
     - Fill in details, **print, sign by hand, and scan**.

  3. **Email Request to Secretariat:**
     - **To:** `amm.disi@unitn.it`, `iecs.school@unitn.it`
     - **CC:** Your advisor
     - **Attach:**
       - Signed FORM-A
       - Event Invoice
       - Invitation letter OR screenshot of event webpage (must show location, dates, fee amount, organizer contact email)
     - ⚠️ *Note: Payments are processed on **Tuesdays**.*

- [ ] **4. Request Advance Payment (Optional)**
  - On the portal, enter a realistic estimate of expected costs.
  - You will receive ~75% of the estimated amount approximately 1 week before departure.

---

## 🧳 Phase 2: After Your Trip

- [ ] **1. Upload Expenses on Portal**
  - Access [UniTn Mission Portal](https://www5.unitn.it/Missioni).
  - Upload each cost line item with proof:
    - **Travel:** Booking confirmation, credit card statement (*estratto conto*), and digital/paper tickets.
    - **Accommodation:** Booking confirmation, credit card statement (*estratto conto*), itemized hotel bill.
    - **Meals:** Itemized paper receipts showing ordered items (always ask for a split bill).
      - *Layout:* Arrange chronologically, tape/staple onto A4 paper. See `Istruzioni_documentazione_liquidazione_missioni.pdf` in `Documents/`.
    - **Proof of Attendance:** Certificate of attendance or equivalent proof.
     - ⚠️ *Note: Paper bills also have to be scanned and uploaded on the Portal

- [ ] **2. Submit Physical Paperwork**
  1. Complete digital submission on the portal.
  2. Put all original physical receipts and printed docs into an envelope addressed to **Ufficio Missioni Polo Collina**.
  3. Drop envelope into the mailbox at **Povo 2, 1st Floor** (next to printer across from secretariat).
  4. *Pre-compiled envelopes:* Available in Room 157 (Segreteria Amministrativa, Povo 2, 1st floor) or print `Documents/Missioni_etichetta.docx`.

- [ ] **3. Reimbursement Processing**
  - See you next at the fiscal period!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do I find the fund code for my budget or project?**
- **Personal Budget:** Check [DISI DSC](https://dsc.disi.unitn.it/). If missing, contact the IECS secretariat.
- **Project Budget:** Ask your supervisor directly for the project code.
