# 🎓 PhD Qualifying Examination Guide

> **Note:** Guidelines for the [IECS Doctoral School Qualifying Exam](https://iecs.unitn.it/enrolled-student/phd-career) (2026–2027).  
> A step-by-step checklist covering submission deadlines, formatting rules, and presentation logistics.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of the Examination

- **Evaluation:** Helps advisors and the Doctoral School assess your potential to complete a PhD thesis and identify early warning signals.
- **Experience:** Provides early practice for formal academic presentations and defenses.

---

## 📋 Checklist & Submission Timeline

- [ ] **Phase 0: Fill [DSC Tool](http://dsc.disi.unitn.it/) (September)**
  1. Check you personal info
  2. Fill the Student's report & Advisor's assessment
     Things to include in the sections:
   Education in research and innovation:  i.e, courses that you attended
   Development of the specific research and \ or innovation:
  - BIBLIOGRAPHIC RESEARCH: i.e., paper read, state-of-the-art research performed
  - INDIVIDUAL AND TEAM WORK: i.e,. papers written, conferences, lab work, any activity related to your individual research work.

- [ ] **Phase 1: Abstract & Keyword Submission (September – October)**
  1. **Account Setup:** Register an account at [openreview.net/signup](https://openreview.net/signup) and complete email verification.
  2. **Portal Submission:** Access the OpenReview UniTn IECS Portal.
  3. **Form Completion:** Select **"University of Trento - IECS Qualifying Exam Submission"**, complete all required fields, and submit.

- [ ] **Phase 2: Research Proposal Submission (November)**
  1. **Final Upload:** Upload your finalized, comprehensive research proposal on OpenReview.
  2. ⚠️ *Academic Suspensions:* If granted an official suspension during your programme, your deadline extends by the corresponding duration. The Secretariat will issue an updated individual schedule.

---

## 📄 Research Proposal Drafting Guidelines

### Document Constraints
- **Length:** Maximum 10 pages.
- **Format:** Standard ACM two-column layout *(use the template that you can find in Documents/PhDQualifyingTemplate)*.

### Required Structure
1. **Contextualization:** Research problem background, importance, and impact.
2. **State of the Art:** Review of existing research surrounding the problem.
3. **Problem Specification:** Specific research problem addressed, highlighting current limitations in existing literature.
4. **Methodology:** Proposed approach and methods to solve the problem.
5. **Preliminary Results:** Any early findings, benchmarks, or initial progress.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Failure to submit the proposal or submitting a document that does not meet the School's evaluation criteria will result in failing the exam.

---

## 💻 Examination Session & Logistics (November – January)

- **Format:** Formal presentation delivered online via **Zoom**.
- **Timing:** Exams run from mid-November to mid-January. *Examinations start as early as mid-November, so complete all preparations prior to this month.*
- **Official Invitation:** Once appointed, you will receive an official invitation detailing the date, time, link, and committee members.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**What happens if I have an unavoidable scheduling conflict?**
- If unavailable due to documented reasons, obtain prior approval from your advisor and notify the Secretariat immediately.

**What happens if I do not pass the examination?**
- Doctoral students who do not pass the initial session are required to retake the examination in a subsequent session.
